Sign in

Sign in with Google to:

  • Log your catches, marks and fishing sessions on the Map (tap + Session and + Catch in Live mode), then edit, copy or delete them — only you see them.
  • See your catch reports on the Reports tab.
  • Add your homes on the Map, so Week Ahead trip times and Live's Home By are worked out from home.
  • Set your own set-up, pack-up and travel times for any location, and — if your plan includes extra locations — add locations of your own.
  • Keep your own location groups and fishing mark lists here in Settings.
  • Have your Week Ahead filters, favourites, planned trips and other settings follow you to every device.

Without signing in you can still browse the Week Ahead and Map conditions for the shared locations.

The pick-from options used when logging a fishing mark (a catch or a point of interest) out on the water — species, bait, weather/tide/water conditions, gear. Saved immediately to your account as you add, remove, or edit them — no separate Save step any more.
