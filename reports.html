Tide clock

When in the tide do you catch fish? Every session is laid onto one full tide cycle (both highs and both lows), measured in hours since the lower low water (LLW), about 25 hours in all. The blue curve is a simulated typical tide built from the average of your sessions' tides, with the differences between the lower and higher highs and lows exaggerated so they stand out (it shows the shape, not real heights), and with the LLW, LHW (lower high), HLW (higher low) and HHW (higher high) marked. Grey columns are how long you fished at each point, coloured columns are catches (by species), and the red line is catches per hour of fishing. Effort follows the date range in the filters; catches follow all the filters. Tide sizes and timings vary from day to day, and thin columns are noisy, so the line only appears where you've fished at least an hour.
