Import from a device export

Pick a Garmin GPX or a Lowrance .usr (format 6) file exported from your sounder's own "export waypoints" option. Every waypoint is matched against what's already in data/marks.json before anything is saved — spots the device saved more than once (a common thing chartplotters do when you drift back over the same mark) are merged into one, and anything within 20m of a mark already tracked is shown but left unchecked, so nothing gets imported twice. Review and edit below, then import only what you actually want kept.
